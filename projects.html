Branding & Marketing Project Cagliari, Sardinia

Black Sun Era — Branding & Marketing Project

Black Sun Era was a creative initiative developed in Cagliari, Sardinia, Italy, with the aim of creating a streetwear clothing line alongside an event platform supporting the local and international hip-hop music scene. The concept brought together fashion, music and community, creating a brand identity designed to connect with a young, culturally engaged audience. I developed the brand identity and visual direction across multiple touchpoints, with a particular focus on creating a distinctive and consistent identity for a Gen Z and Millennial audience.

Brand Identity & Logo Concept

The logo was designed around the idea of a globe, representing community, connection and the brand's relationship with a wider international culture. At the centre of the globe is a stylised sun inspired by traditional Sardinian art, with geometric triangular forms and diamond motifs characteristic of Sardinian decorative patterns. The black-and-white colour palette was intentionally selected to communicate the brand's streetwear and hip-hop identity while giving it a serious, refined, underground aesthetic, and provides flexibility across clothing, digital platforms, advertising and event materials.

Marketing & Brand Application

Website concept and digital layouts, social media content, online advertising concepts, offline advertising and promotional materials, photography and visual content, branded promotional concepts. Each touchpoint was developed with brand consistency in mind across channels.

Brand Identity & Communications Anglia Ruskin University

Marketing Society — Brand Identity & Communications

Since the beginning of my second academic year (2023) at Anglia Ruskin University in London, I have been volunteering as Treasurer of the Marketing Society, which supports Business and Marketing students through seminars, networking events, and short courses.

I took the initiative to develop and strengthen the society's brand identity — designing the logo, selecting typography, and developing branded materials such as banners, promotional assets, and merchandise to increase brand awareness across campus.

I also implemented a consistent visual communication approach across the society's promotional activities and digital channels, creating event materials including social media posts, reels, captions, digital assets, and presentation slides — designed to promote events, increase student engagement, and encourage participation across Instagram and LinkedIn.

Brand Identity & Web Concept Chelsea, London

Farm Fetch — Brand Identity & Web Concept

Designed the logo and key visual elements that define the brand identity of Farm Fetch, a premium culinary brand based in Chelsea, London. This section showcases the logo development process, including its structure, variations, and supporting graphic assets. The project also includes a homepage design concept integrating visual and informative elements that reflect Farm Fetch's premium positioning and focus on quality, authenticity, and culinary excellence. I also developed Instagram visual content through photography, aligned with the brand identity.

"Farm Fetch's mission is to bring the beauty of Italian, Spanish and French food culture to the UK and to throw a spotlight on small artisan producers who continually strive to preserve their local heritage."

The logo communicates elegance and quality through the matt gold colour and the simplified capital beneath the word 'Farm Fetch'. The brand name is well readable thanks to a personalised font, reinforcing the sense of professionalism and brand recognition. The symbol below the text draws inspiration from the capitals of the columns outside the store, taking advantage of the elegant architecture that defines both the store and its atmosphere.

Beauty Pie — Digital Marketing Campaign Proposal: "Free to be Beauty" (2025)

Developed a 3,200-word comprehensive digital marketing proposal for Beauty Pie's "Free to be Beauty" campaign, focusing on launching the new Japanfusion Un-Genius Lift Elixir alongside the new Beauty Pie Native Mobile App (iOS & Android). The campaign incentivises app downloads with an exclusive 10% E-code discount, aiming for 75,000 downloads and a 20% in-app purchase conversion rate.

Audit & Strategic Insights

Utilised Seoptimer.com and SimilarWeb.com to conduct a rigorous digital audit. Identified critical mobile usability deficiencies (Grade D, Mobile Speed Score 24/100, LCP 11.4s, TTI 28.8s, 43.61% bounce rate) despite 82.02% of traffic originating from mobile devices. Benchmarked against The Ordinary using Porter's Five Forces, leveraging Beauty Pie's membership model and native app ecosystem to justify premium positioning.

Frameworks Applied
SOSTAC, RACE, PESO & SMART
Total Campaign Budget
£7,320,000 (10% of 2024 Turnover)
Audit Tools Used
Seoptimer, SimilarWeb, SERP
Key Channels & Creators
TikTok, IG, Emma Jane, Bambi
Implementation & Timetable

Structured a 6-month campaign calendar mapped to the Product Life Cycle (PLC), balancing Paid media in early launch stages before shifting to Earned/Owned channels (SEO, AI machine-learning retargeting, Mailchimp email automation, and UGC hashtags #BeautyPieHealth, #BeautyPieIngredients, #BeautyPieDiscount).

Lidl GB vs Aldi UK — Social Media Competitive Analysis

Conducted a social media competitive analysis of Lidl GB and Aldi UK to demonstrate the application of marketing data analysis methodologies to social media performance, using a manually collected sample of publicly available data (as API/proprietary analytics access was unavailable).

Marketing Objective & Metrics

Marketing Objective: Assess how Lidl GB could increase social media engagement and strengthen brand perception compared with Aldi UK — identifying which content types drive higher engagement, differences in content performance and posting frequency, audience response patterns, and opportunities for Lidl to improve its social content strategy.

Engagement Rate KPI
Interactions ÷ Followers
Sentiment Ratio KPI
Comment sentiment in R
Dataset Size
120 Posts (IG, FB, X)
Content Themes
Brand Value, Events, Memes, Product
Methodology & Analytical Tools

Methodology: Manually collected and analysed 20 posts per platform (Instagram, Facebook, X) per brand — 120 posts total from the 2025–2026 period. Categorised content into four themes (Brand Value, Events, Memes, Product) and calculated average engagement rate and posting frequency per category/platform.
Tools Used: Microsoft Excel (data collection, organisation, categorisation, engagement-rate calculations); R Environment (sentiment analysis, comment classification, sentiment-ratio calculation).
